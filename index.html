How this works

Methodology & limitations.

A transparent explanation of the model behind the rankings, based on the original RotoGrinders research.

01 / DATASET

124 coaches across four leagues

The study includes 32 NFL, 30 NBA, 30 MLB and 32 NHL coaches. Three photographs were assessed per coach, for 372 total image-level analyses.

02 / MEASUREMENT

Six facial proportions

Computer vision was used to compare facial length versus width, facial thirds, eye width and gap, nose-to-mouth and mouth-to-chin distance, eye-to-mouth distance relative to face length, and pupil spacing relative to face width.

03 / SCORING

Averages—not extra points

The six individual scores contribute equally to each image-level result. Neoclassical and landmark-proportion composite scores summarize those measures; they are not counted twice. The three image results are averaged per coach.

What the results do—and don't—mean: The scores reflect alignment with a chosen facial-proportion framework. They are not objective statements about beauty or coaching performance. Camera angle, lighting, expression, cropping and photo selection can affect the measurements. Source: RotoGrinders' full study.
